About {{ site_name }}

We are an avocado trading business based in Nairobi, buying from smallholder farms and packhouses across Kenya's highlands and supplying retailers, hotels, juice processors, exporters and households.

Our model is simple: transparent prices per kilogram, fruit graded before it leaves the packhouse, and delivery you can plan around. We started with weekly deliveries to groceries in Nairobi and now move several tonnes a week across four counties.

What we look for in every consignment

  • Oil content of 10-12% at harvest, checked before dispatch
  • Firm fruit with clean skin and no bruising
  • Consistent sizing per carton so buyers can price confidently
  • Traceability: farm, harvest date and pack date on request

Talk to us before you order

Volumes change daily during the season. Message us for today's availability, or request a formal quote and we will send it to your account.
