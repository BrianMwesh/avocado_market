Sourced from Kenyan highlands

We work with smallholder farms and packhouses in Murang'a, Kiambu, Nyeri, Meru, Kisii and Trans Nzoia. Fruit is harvested at 10-12% oil content, field-sorted, and packed the same day to protect shelf life.

Every consignment is graded for size and appearance before dispatch. Buyers receive batch details on request, and recurring buyers can set a weekly standing order.

  • Grade A export quality and Grade B local trade
  • 10kg, 18kg and 20kg vented cartons, or 90kg sacks
  • Delivery in Nairobi within 24 hours, countrywide courier in 1-3 days
  • Payment on delivery for local orders, or invoice terms for registered buyers
